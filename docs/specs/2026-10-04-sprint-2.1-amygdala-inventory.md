## Goal

The amygdala module's vocabulary, before literature extraction starts (sprints 2.2–2.3): which regions claims may name in each species, the neuron types they may name, and the names papers use for both.

## Names papers use (for the extractor's normaliser)

Region names are the hard part of extraction (only 63 % of region mentions resolved to a lexicon in WhiteText; plan Part 2, section 4). These are the traps in amygdala papers; sprint 2.3 turns them into the normaliser's rules.

| Name in a paper | Means | Map to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BLA (Allen, most mouse papers) | Basolateral amygdalar nucleus | MBA:295 |
| BLA (Paxinos atlases, mouse and rat) | Basolateral amygdaloid nucleus, **anterior** part | MBA:303 in mouse; UBERON:0002887 in rat |
| BLA, "basolateral amygdala" (many behavioural papers) | The whole basolateral complex: lateral, basal and basomedial nuclei | UBERON:0006107 in rat; in mouse the subject is the complex, so prefer the paper's own nucleus when it gives one |
| BL, B, BA, "basal nucleus" (Pitkänen's rat nomenclature) | Basal amygdaloid nucleus | UBERON:0002887 in rat; MBA:295 in mouse |
| AB, BM, BMA, "accessory basal nucleus" | Basomedial (accessory basal) nucleus | MBA:319; UBERON:0002889 in rat, as UBERON's MBA bridge maps it (UBERON also has UBERON:0002885, accessory basal amygdaloid nucleus) |
| LA, La, LaDL, LaVL, LaVM | Lateral nucleus, or its dorsolateral, ventrolateral and ventromedial parts | MBA:131; UBERON:0002886 in rat (UBERON also has terms for the parts, UBERON:0023415–0023417, outside its amygdala hierarchy) |
| CeA, CeN, ACe | Central nucleus | MBA:536; UBERON:0002883 |
| CeL, CeC, CeM (Paxinos) | Lateral, capsular and medial divisions of the central nucleus | MBA:551, MBA:544, MBA:559 in mouse; UBERON:0002883 in rat |
| ITC, ITCs, IM, I, mITC, lITC, "paracapsular cells" | Intercalated cell masses | MBA:1105; UBERON:0002884 |
| MeA, Me, MeAD, MePD, MePV | Medial nucleus or its parts | MBA:403; UBERON:0002892 |
| "amygdala" alone | Unspecified | UBERON:0001876 only if the paper is no more specific; flag for the verifier |

The neuron types' synonyms follow the same conventions. "CeL-off" neurons are mostly PKC-delta-expressing (Haubensak et al. 2010), so "CeL-off" is a synonym of the CEAl PKC-delta type, not an identity.

## Open questions for the maintainer

1. **Where the lexicon lives.** The table above is prose. Sprint 2.3 needs it machine-readable: a schema class in `data/` (a schema change), or a resource beside the extractor's prompt in `agents/`?
2. **Neuron-type evidence.** Should neuron types cite their defining papers (a schema change), or is the evidence only the claims that name them?
3. **Cre lines.** The Allen Cre-line experiments (ADR 0010) label these types, such as Prkcd-Cre and Sst-Cre. Should Cre-line projections become neuron-type claims, and against which of these records?
4. **Gold set.** These types and the region traps above are a natural frame for the gold set (sprint 0.5), but curating it stays with the maintainer.
