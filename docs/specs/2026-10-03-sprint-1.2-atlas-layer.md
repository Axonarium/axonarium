## Goal

Claims name regions in pinned atlas versions. This sprint pins the mouse, human and rat atlases, loads their region hierarchies at build time with BrainGlobe, maps regions to UBERON from UBERON's own published bridges, and makes the build fail when data names a region its atlas doesn't have. The site shows region names instead of bare IDs.

## What the atlases offer (checked 3 October 2026)

| Atlas | BrainGlobe | Regions | Amygdala | IDs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Allen Mouse CCFv3, 2017 annotation | `allen_mouse_25um` 3.1 | 856 | Full: LA, BLA (a, p, v), BMA, PA, CEA (c, l, m), MEA, COA, IA, AAA | `MBA:` (Allen graph 1) |
| Allen Human Reference Atlas, 3D 2020 (Ding et al. 2016 ontology) | `allen_human_500um` 3.1 | 208 | AMY, BLN, BL, BM, Co, Me, AHA, AAA | `DHBA:` (Allen graph 16), new in the schema |
| Waxholm Space SD rat, v4 (Kleven et al. 2023) | `whs_sd_rat_39um` 3.0 | 314 | One region: "Amygdaloid area, unspecified" | none |

Rat amygdala nuclei aren't delineated in any open rat atlas BrainGlobe offers, so rat claims name UBERON terms. The Waxholm atlas is pinned for its meshes (sprint 3.3b) but contributes no region IDs; it gets a prefix when a rat atlas with amygdala nuclei arrives.

## Decisions

| Decision | Choice |
| --- | --- |
| Region data | Read at build time from BrainGlobe (`brainglobe-atlasapi` 3.0.2, BSD-3), never committed: ADR 0005 keeps Allen content out of the repo; Waxholm is CC BY-SA |
| Pinning | Each atlas record names its BrainGlobe atlas and, in `extra.brainglobe.atlas_version`, the atlas data version. The build stops if BrainGlobe serves another version: an update is a reviewed change |
| UBERON mappings | From UBERON's published bridges (`uberon-bridge-to-mba.owl`, `uberon-bridge-to-dhba.owl`, CC BY 3.0), read with rdflib: a region whose `owl:equivalentClass` is a UBERON class restricted to the species maps to that class. No hand-curated mapping |
| Unknown regions | A build rule: every `MBA:` or `DHBA:` region named in data (claims, neuron types) must be a region of the atlas it names, or the build stops |
| Dumps | Atlas-derived regions go to the database (the live site may show them, with the atlas citation) but not into the dumps, per ADR 0005 |

## Schema 0.3.0

`DHBA` prefix (`https://purl.brain-bican.org/ontology/dhbao/DHBA_`, as in the Bioregistry). Region IDs and atlas-region references accept `DHBA:` beside `MBA:` and `HBA:`; such references must name their atlas. The online checks look `DHBA:` IDs up in Allen structure graph 16.

## Data

Three atlas records in `data/entities/atlases/`: `allen-mouse-ccf-2017`, `allen-human-3d-2020`, `waxholm-sd-rat-v4`, each with its BrainGlobe pin and its citation (a new `Atlas.citation` slot, column and Alembic migration).

## Build

`python -m build` loads every atlas with a BrainGlobe name and a region prefix (`allen_mouse` → `MBA`, `allen_human` → `DHBA`): one region row per structure, with name, acronym, atlas, parent and the UBERON term from the bridge. `--no-atlases` skips this (tests, offline work). The build prints, per atlas, the amygdala regions that resolve: the structures whose UBERON term is the amygdala (UBERON:0001876) or one of its nuclei used in claims (basolateral complex, lateral, basal, basomedial, central, medial, cortical).

## Site

Region names next to IDs on the explore, edge and claim pages; an `/atlases` page listing each atlas and its amygdala regions with UBERON links; the atlas citation wherever Allen-derived names appear.

## Tests

- Unit (no network): the bridge query on small synthetic OWL fixtures (equivalence, a nested intersection that isn't exact, a non-UBERON class); region rows from synthetic structures; the pin check; the unknown-region rule.
- Integration (network, CI's rebuild step): the real build loads both Allen atlases, and every amygdala region the bridges map resolves in its atlas.
