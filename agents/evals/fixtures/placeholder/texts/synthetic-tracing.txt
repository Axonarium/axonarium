Abstract. This synthetic text exists only to test Axonarium's eval harness. Nothing in it is evidence.

Results. We injected the anterograde tracer PHA-L into the basolateral amygdalar nucleus (BLA) of adult C57BL/6 mice (n = 6). Labelled axons and terminal boutons were dense in the lateral division of the central amygdala (CeL; Fig. 1B) and moderate in the nucleus accumbens (Fig. 1C). No labelled fibres were found in the prelimbic cortex in any animal, although the same sections showed labelling elsewhere (Fig. 1D).

To test the central amygdala's outputs, we injected the retrograde tracer cholera toxin B into the periaqueductal gray of mice (n = 4). Retrogradely labelled neurons were found in the medial division of the central amygdala (CeM; Fig. 2A).
